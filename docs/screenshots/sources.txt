The images in the screenshots, all in the public domain, from Wikimedia Commons.

NASA's photos: by NASA or its astronauts, not copyrighted, per NASA's media guidelines
(https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/); none is credited to a third party.
The paintings and prints: by artists who died before 1900 (Hokusai 1849, Van Gogh 1890, Turner
1851, Vermeer 1675, Friedrich 1840, Seurat 1891, Constable 1837, Cole 1848, Millet 1875), each
marked Public domain on its Commons page. Each was downloaded at Commons' largest thumbnail,
scaled to 2400 pixels at most and stripped of metadata; the edit shot used The Hay Wain scaled to
1200 pixels.
